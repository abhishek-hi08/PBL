# Germany Travel Brochure & National Transport Plan

A modern, high-polish travel brochure and national rail transport planning web platform for **Germany**, featuring a hero interface inspired directly by your design mockup.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. Pixel-Matched Hero Experience
- **Identical Layout to Reference**:
  - Warm coral framed canvas with dark frosted-glass container (`border-radius: 32px`).
  - Top navigation bar featuring the circular coral **"G"** badge, frosted-glass search pill, and menu pill with a 9-dot grid icon.
  - Left vertical interactive timeline with active glowing coral indicator.
  - Bold typography: **"Germany"** with subtext and coral **"Explore Germany →"** action button.
  - Bottom-left **"↓ Scroll to discover"** cue.
  - 3 floating vertical glass cards on the right with location pins, region subtitles, and active focus styling.
  - Prev/Next circular coral buttons (`←` / `→`) and **01 ———— 05** animated progress indicator.
  - Bottom scrolling ticker: *Scenic High-Speed ICE Railway • Fairy-Tale Neuschwanstein Castle • Deutschlandticket Unlimited Pass • Romantic Rhine Gorge...*
- **5 Crossfading Destination Slides**:
  1. **Bavaria (Bayern)**: Neuschwanstein Castle, Munich, Zugspitze, Lake Königssee.
  2. **Berlin & Brandenburg**: Brandenburg Gate, Museum Island, Reichstag Dome.
  3. **The Black Forest (Schwarzwald)**: Triberg waterfalls, Baden-Baden thermal spas.
  4. **The Romantic Rhine & Moselle**: Cliffside castles, Lorelei rock, terraced vineyards.
  5. **Hamburg & The Hanseatic North**: Speicherstadt canals, Elbphilharmonie.

---

### 2. Complete German National Transport Plan
- **Deutschlandticket (D-Ticket / €49–€58 Pass)**:
  - Deep-dive guide explaining unlimited travel on all Regional-Express (RE), Regionalbahn (RB), S-Bahn, U-Bahn, trams, buses, and harbor ferries nationwide.
  - Practical tourist purchasing instructions and cancellation tips.
- **Interactive Rail Route & Travel Time Calculator**:
  - Select origin and destination (e.g. *Berlin ⇄ Munich*, *Munich ⇄ Neuschwanstein*, *Frankfurt ⇄ Heidelberg*, *Cologne ⇄ Rhine Gorge*).
  - Compare high-speed ICE times vs regional trains, rail distance, departure frequency, and live Deutschlandticket validity.
- **Top 4 Scenic Rail Journeys**:
  - *The Black Forest Railway (Schwarzwaldbahn)* — 39 tunnels and viaducts through pine gorges.
  - *The Middle Rhine Gorge Valley Line* — past 20+ castles along the riverbanks.
  - *Bavarian Alpine & Karwendel Line* — direct access to Zugspitze.
  - *Harz Narrow-Gauge Brocken Steam Railway* — historic coal-fired locomotives.
- **Transit Pass Comparison Matrix**:
  - Side-by-side comparison between the Deutschlandticket, German Rail Pass (Eurail), DB Sparpreis tickets, and Länder-Tickets (Bayern-Ticket).
- **Practical DB Travel Etiquette**:
  - Quiet Zones (*Ruhebereich*), DB Navigator carriage formation (*Wagenreihung*), luggage lockers (*Schließfächer*), ticket validation (*Entwerter*), and Bordbistro dining.

## 🚀 How to Open and View

Since the project uses vanilla HTML5, CSS3, and modern JavaScript with zero dependencies, you can open it immediately:

1. **Directly in your Browser**:
   Double-click `index.html` or open in Terminal:
   ```bash
   open index.html
   ```

2. **Via Local Server (Optional)**:
   ```bash
   python3 -m http.server 8000
   ```
   Then open `http://localhost:8000` in Chrome, Safari, or Arc.
